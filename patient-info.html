Patient & Family Information

Clear, straightforward guidance to help you navigate a visit, admission, or enquiry.

Medical Centre Patients

Outpatient Visits (OPD)

Please bring any previous medical records, investigation reports, and a current list of medications. Contact us in advance to arrange an appointment or confirm availability.

What to bring

  • • Previous medical records and reports
  • • Current medications list
  • • Valid identification

Inpatient Admission

Our team will guide you through the admission documentation. We will provide a list of personal items to bring for your stay.

Suggested items

  • • Personal clothing and toiletries
  • • Medications and prescription details
  • • Family contact details
  • • Identity documents

Senior Care Home

Arranging a Visit

Choosing a care home is a significant decision. We encourage families to schedule a visit so you can experience our environment and discuss specific needs with our care coordinator.

Request a Visit

Admission Process

Before admission, a care assessment is conducted to ensure we can meet all requirements safely and comfortably. Our team will guide you through documentation and answer all questions.

Documents typically required

  • • Identity and address proof
  • • Medical history and current prescriptions
  • • Next-of-kin and emergency contact details

Family Visits

Family visits are welcomed and encouraged. Please contact us to confirm visiting arrangements and current hours.

Family Communication

We keep families informed about their relative's wellbeing. Our team is available to discuss any concerns. Please contact us for details of our communication process.

Emergency guidance: This facility does not operate as an emergency service. For urgent medical situations, contact 112 or proceed to the nearest emergency department immediately.
