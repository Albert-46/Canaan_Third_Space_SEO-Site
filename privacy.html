Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Canaan Third Space Senior Care Home collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through this public website. As a healthcare and caregiving institution, we treat privacy and data security seriously.

This policy relates to information submitted via the public website only. The care and medical records of residents and patients are governed by separate, sector-appropriate policies and are never stored on or transmitted through this website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide, for example when you complete our online enquiry form. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • The nature of your enquiry
  • Any additional information you include in your message

We do not automatically collect sensitive medical or health information when you browse this website.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information submitted through the enquiry form is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We will not use your information for marketing or pass it to third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

3. How Enquiries Are Submitted

The enquiry form on this website is currently configured to open your local email application, pre-filled with the details you have entered. This means your enquiry is sent using your own email provider directly to our team's email address. The website itself does not store or transmit your message.

By using this method, your data is processed by your chosen email provider under that provider's own privacy policy. We recommend you review your email provider's privacy terms if you have concerns.

4. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

If a WhatsApp contact link is displayed on this website and you choose to use it, your communication will be processed by WhatsApp LLC (Meta Platforms, Inc.) under their privacy policy. We do not control that service or how it handles your data.

We do not currently embed third-party social media, advertising, or tracking scripts on this website.

5. Analytics and Cookies

This website may use essential cookies to maintain basic functionality (such as navigation state). We do not currently use aggressive third-party analytics or tracking services. If analytics tools are introduced in future, this policy will be updated and users informed.

6. Data Retention

We retain information contained in email enquiries only for as long as is necessary to respond to your enquiry and to maintain reasonable records. If you would like your information removed from our email records, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you as a result of your communication with us
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, provided it does not conflict with legal or regulatory obligations

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through our Contact page or by email at .

9. Disclaimer

This policy has not been reviewed by a qualified legal professional. It reflects current practices for this website only and does not constitute legal advice. The organisation may engage professional legal review before the website is published.
