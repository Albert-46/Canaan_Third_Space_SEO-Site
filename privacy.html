Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. As a healthcare and caregiving institution, we treat your privacy and data security with the utmost seriousness.

Data Collection & Cookies

When you use our public website, we do not automatically collect sensitive medical information. Any basic contact information (such as name, email, or phone number) provided voluntarily through our enquiry forms is processed directly via email client and not stored in a public database. We use essential cookies to maintain site functionality.

Contact-Form Handling

Enquiries submitted via this website are currently configured to open your local email application directly, sending data securely through your own email provider to our reception team.

Third-Party Services & Analytics

We do not employ aggressive third-party trackers. Minimal, anonymized analytics may be used strictly to monitor the technical performance and accessibility of our website.

User Rights

You have the right to request information regarding any personal data we may hold as a result of your communication with us, and to request its deletion, provided it does not conflict with legal medical record-keeping requirements.
