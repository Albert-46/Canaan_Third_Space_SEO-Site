Home Care Centre

Medical services for you and your family.

Professional healthcare delivered in a calm, clean, and compassionate environment. Our care centre provides the services families in our community rely on.

This page provides general information about our services. Please contact us to discuss your specific needs. This is not a source of medical advice. No diagnosis, treatment, or clinical guidance is offered here.

Service 01

OPD Consultation

Our outpatient department provides scheduled consultations in a private, calm setting. Whether you are visiting for a routine check or specific concern, our team ensures a thorough and respectful appointment.

Appointments are available during our regular operating hours. Families are welcome to accompany patients.

  • Scheduled consultations
  • General health assessments
  • Referral for specialist review when needed
  • Families welcome during appointments
Enquire About Consultations
Service 02

Inpatient Admission

For patients who require closer observation or a period of supervised care, our inpatient facility offers comfortable accommodation with attentive nursing care and regular medical review.

We maintain a calm ward environment that supports rest and recovery, with family visiting hours designed to keep loved ones connected throughout a stay.

  • Comfortable, supervised inpatient rooms
  • Regular medical review by our care team
  • Nursing care & daily assistance
  • Family visiting hours & communication
Enquire About Admissions
Service 03

Laboratory Services

Our on-site laboratory supports diagnostic needs for both outpatient and inpatient care. Results are handled with confidentiality and communicated to treating clinicians appropriately.

Patients can be referred for tests by our care team. Please contact us to understand available tests and collection procedures.

  • On-site diagnostic testing
  • Results communicated to treating clinician
  • Professional handling with confidentiality
  • Referral-based access
