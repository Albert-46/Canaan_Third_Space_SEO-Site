Information Guide

Information for patients and families.

Practical guidance to help patients, residents, and their families navigate a visit, admission, or enquiry as smoothly as possible.

This page provides general guidance only and does not constitute medical advice. For specific medical questions, please consult a qualified clinician.

For Patients

Outpatient Visits (OPD)

Please contact us in advance to enquire about consultation availability. Arrive a few minutes early and bring any previous medical records or test results relevant to your visit.

Inpatient Admission

Admissions are arranged through our care team. Please contact us to discuss availability, requirements, and any specific needs before arrival.

What to Bring

  • Valid government-issued identification
  • Referral letter (if applicable)
  • Previous test results or medical records
  • List of current medications
  • Contact details of a family member

During Your Stay

Our nursing team is available throughout the day. Please do not hesitate to ask questions or raise concerns. We are here to help.

For Residents & Families

Admission Enquiry

Families interested in residential accommodation for a senior family member are welcome to contact us to discuss availability, the admission process, and a campus visit.

Visiting Hours

We welcome family visits during designated visiting hours. Please contact us to confirm current visiting arrangements, as these may vary.

Family Communication

Our care team maintains open communication with families about the wellbeing of residents. Questions and concerns can always be raised by contacting the care team directly.

What to Bring for Residency

  • Personal clothing and comfort items
  • Identification and official documents
  • Medical records and prescriptions
  • Emergency contact information
  • Any assistive devices (walking aids, etc.)
